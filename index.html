The Process

Most work is done in Autodesk Fusion. If your team runs SolidWorks, Inventor, Creo, Onshape, NX, or another system, provide a license or seat and I'll work directly in your environment, using your templates, title blocks, and file conventions.

How it works

  1. Send what you haveA sketch, photo, sample part, old print, or STEP file.
  2. Get a fixed quoteA set price and a delivery date before any work starts.
  3. Review a draftMark it up; revisions are handled before anything is final.
  4. Receive your filesNative CAD, STEP, and a PDF drawing set

About the company

About DAC

DAC is a contract CAD and drafting company based in San Luis Obispo, California, working with machine shops and hardware teams across the U.S.

We turn sketches, sample parts, old prints, and shop measurements into clean 3D models and machinist-ready drawings, with GD&T per ASME Y14.5 on every print. Every job starts with a fixed quote and a delivery date, and every drawing is checked for manufacturability
